## Our Pricing Strategy

### Free Tier (Forever)
- 5 swing analyses per month
- Basic fault detection (shoulder tilt + head up only)
- Last 10 sessions in history
- Tempo chart included
- No export

### Pro Plan (~$79 CAD/year = ~$6.58/month)
**Undercutting GolfFix by ~37%**
- Unlimited swing analyses
- All 3 fault checks per angle (full diagnostic engine)
- Full history (unlimited)
- Score trend chart + fault frequency tracking
- Video export with overlay annotations
- DTL + Face-On angle gating (already built)
- Audio coach (already built)
- Priority support

### Future Premium Plan (~$129 CAD/year)
- Everything in Pro
- Range Mode (continuous auto-clip)
- Pro swing side-by-side comparison
- Monthly report with club filtering
- Club type selector (Driver vs Iron vs Wedge)

### Trial Structure
- **14-day free trial** on Pro plan (match GolfFix exactly)
- No credit card required to start trial (conversion best practice)
- Cancel anytime
- After trial: downgrade to Free tier (not cut off entirely)

## Paywall Trigger Points

Show the paywall/upgrade screen when user:
1. Tries to run analysis #6 in a month (Free limit)
2. Taps "Export" on results screen
3. Tries to view sessions beyond #10 in History
4. Taps any locked feature (Range Mode, Monthly Report)

**Never show paywall on first session.** Let them feel the product first.

## Revenue Model Notes

- **Target**: 1,000 paid subscribers at $79 CAD/year = $79,000 ARR
- **Conversion rate target**: 5% of free users → paid (industry average is 2-5%)
- **LTV**: Golf is a lifestyle — subscribers tend to retain 2-3 years
- **No ads ever**: Part of our brand promise. Premium feel.
- **App Store cut**: Apple takes 30% in year 1, 15% in year 2+ (Small Business Program)
  - At $79 CAD: we net ~$55 CAD in year 1, ~$67 in year 2+
