# Landing Page Copy

Two full landing page treatments: **OnPlane** (required) and **Piped** (selected as the strongest alternate).

**Why Piped over RightPlane or TruePlane?**
Piped earns more attention at the hero than either plane-variant alternate. The other names telegraph "golf instruction app" and settle into a predictable category. Piped is punchy, confident, and slightly unexpected — the same qualities that make a good golf shot memorable. The insider-coded language is a feature here, not a bug: the target audience (average-to-technical golfers who want to improve) will immediately understand it, and that recognition creates purchase intent. It positions the brand as made by golfers, not made for golfers — a meaningful distinction. The risk profile (trademark, SEO) is manageable at this stage and not a reason to choose a safer but duller name.

### Feature Blocks

**Block 1: AI Pose Analysis**

Title: Your body, mapped to the millimeter

OnPlane uses MoveNet, the same pose detection powering elite sports performance tools, to track every joint in your swing frame by frame. It doesn't guess — it sees where your hips, shoulders, and head actually are, then flags what physics says they shouldn't be doing.

**Block 2: Two-Angle Fault Detection**

Title: One angle misses half the picture

Most swing apps ask you to film from one spot. OnPlane films your swing Down the Line and Face-On simultaneously, because a fault that looks fine from the front often shows up clearly from behind. Shoulder tilt, reverse pivot, early extension, head movement — each fault has a signature angle. We look at both.

**Block 3: Tempo + Score Tracking**

Title: A number that actually means something

Your swing score (0–100) reflects real mechanics: fault severity, phase timing, and tempo ratio — not just whether you hit the ball. Track it across sessions and you'll see exactly where you improved and where work remains. Progress stops feeling vague and starts feeling measurable.

## Marketing Update — Shot Shape & SHANK Detection (March 2026)

### Key Differentiator: Shot Shape Identification

Every fault now maps to the shot outcome it causes. This is the language golfers already speak — they don't say "I have early extension", they say "I keep shanking it." OnPlane bridges that gap.

**Fault → Shot Shape Mapping:**
| Fault | Shot Outcomes |
|-------|--------------|
| Shoulder Tilt at Address | Push, Pull |
| Reverse Pivot | Slice, Push, Topped |
| Head Up at Impact | Thin, Shank, Slice |
| Early Extension | Shank, Chunk, Thin |

### The SHANK is Our Selling Point

A shank (hosel strike sending ball 90° right) is the single most traumatic shot in golf. When our system detects Head Up + Early Extension simultaneously, it fires a RED shank risk alert. This is emotionally resonant in a way no competitor matches.

**Copy angles:**
- "Finally know why you're shanking it." (headline test)
- "Your app diagnosed the shank. Your pro couldn't." (social proof)
- "One swing. Detected: ⚡ SHANK RISK. Fixed in 5 minutes." (results claim)

### Revised Value Proposition Hierarchy

1. **Shank detection** — most emotionally urgent, drives urgency to sign up
2. **Phase-by-phase walkthrough** — 5 crisp result screens, guided coaching
3. **Swing plane arc** — visual proof of over-the-top / on-plane / too-flat
4. **Angle cards** — quantified, data-driven, appeals to technical golfers
5. **Dual-view compare** — "compare today's swing to last month" (future)
